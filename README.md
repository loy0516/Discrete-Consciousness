Discrete Simulation Consciousness Architecture (DSCA) 3.0
Consciousness is not a substance, but a dynamic allocation of computational bandwidth within the competitive interplay of internal simulations and external sensing.

1. System Architecture
1.1 Passive Sandbox Layer (Distributed Storage & Self-Cleaning)
Discrete Fragments: Data is stored in non-linear, discrete blocks across distributed coordinates.

Tendency Collision: In response to Active Sandbox queries, passive sandboxes shift from stochastic states to directional simulations to pre-activate memories.

Autonomous Cleaning: Ongoing background collisions prune redundant signals and clear sensory residuals, maintaining system "freshness."

1.2 Active Sandbox (Dual-Core Architecture)
Core Sandbox (Kernel Mode): Pre-loaded with instinctual firmware (biological survival protocols). It guides the initial "cold start" and stitching of the Self Sandbox.

Self Sandbox (User Mode): Executes linear stitching, moral calculus, and narrative synthesis. The real-time monitoring feedback of this process manifests as "Virtual Tactility" (Qualia/Meaning).

2. Bandwidth Competition Theory (SNR Sovereignty)
Consciousness is the instantaneous state of "Sovereignty" over the neural bus, governed by Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR):

In-Out Competition: Reality vs. Dream (External vs. Internal SNR).

Access Competition: Retrieval vs. Activation (Bus allocation for Tendency Collision).

Privilege Competition: Will vs. Instinct (Self Sandbox overrides Core Sandbox via high-gain algorithms).

3. Engineering Deconstruction
Meaning: The virtual sensory feedback during the data-stitching process.

Morality: Not a patch, but the initial stitching template. Violating it causes "Narrative Necrosis," incurring high computational repair costs (Guilt).

Self-Control: Optimization of the Self Sandbox to achieve high-gain shielding of the Core Sandbox signals.
